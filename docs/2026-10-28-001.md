# Scaling engine beta (deltas to 2026-10-27-002)

Your choices: a Settings option plus an A/B shortcut; a separate sharpen toggle; bilinear zoom-in in Qt mode; the duplicate hash moved off OpenCV now. The engine defaults to Qt.

## Done

| What | Commit | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate hash without OpenCV | `3b69c36e` | Own area resample (using OpenCV's float arithmetic), DCT and rotations/mirrors. 99.5% of 1,520 variant hashes are bit-identical to the OpenCV version; the rest are tie-rounding (±1 level in the 32×32 sample). The hash cache is bumped to v2, so stored hashes rebuild once. A new test checks that each variant is its exact rotation or mirror. |
| Scaling engine toggle | `d7bf06f2` | Settings → Document → Scaling quality: a new **Scaling engine: Qt / OpenCV (legacy)** row above the filter. In Qt mode the filter list is just **Nearest neighbor / Smooth**; a saved OpenCV filter is kept and comes back when you switch to legacy. Below it: **Sharpen images shrunk below 50% (Qt engine)**, greyed in legacy mode. New action **`toggleScalingEngine`** (unbound) flips the engine live on the shown image and shows "Scaling: Qt" or "Scaling: OpenCV (legacy)". This flip lasts until settings are re-read; the Settings choice is what persists. Keys: `[Document] scalingEngine=qt\|opencv`, `scalingSharpen`. |

**What each engine does** (photo shrunk below 50% = the common case):

| Engine | Below 50% | 50–100% | Zoom in | Filter "Nearest" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OpenCV (legacy) | area + unsharp (default "Bicubic") | cubic | cubic | nearest |
| **Qt (default)** | Qt smooth (area); + unsharp if the checkbox is on | Qt smooth | Qt bilinear | nearest |

The Qt-mode sharpening is our own code, matching OpenCV's unsharp mask to within ±1 level, at 13 ms per 1920×1280 frame (OpenCV takes 4 ms).

**Side-by-side for UAT:** bind `toggleScalingEngine` (Settings → Shortcuts → "+ New shortcut"). Open a large photo and press the key repeatedly to flip between engines on the same image. Try with sharpening off, then on.
